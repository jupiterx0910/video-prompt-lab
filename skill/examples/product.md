# Product Example

**Idea:** A premium running shoe hero shot in rain.

**IR focus:** shoe identity, wet pavement, rain impact, controlled tracking camera, reflective material response, 8-second beat structure.

**Routing:** Seedance or Kling for product/commercial motion; respect an explicit user-selected model.

**Expected prompt behavior:** specify material, contact, water displacement, camera path and temporal beats rather than relying on adjectives such as `cinematic` or `epic`.
