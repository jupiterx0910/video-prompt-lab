# Image-to-Video Example

**Idea:** Turn a portrait still into a subtle emotional reaction shot.

**IR focus:** preserve identity and composition, define micro-expression change, head/eye movement, breathing, lighting continuity and restrained camera motion.

**Routing:** Kling or Runway when image-to-video is the primary task.

**Expected prompt behavior:** protect the source image's identity and composition first; introduce only the requested temporal changes.
